Privacy Policy

App: Worm Escape · Developer: Technoor Studio · Last updated: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Worm Escape ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you play the game. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contact list, photos, or precise location.

The following information may be collected automatically by the services listed in section 3:

  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, app version, language, and crash logs.
  • Usage information: anonymous events such as levels played, game modes used, and feature interactions.
  • Advertising identifiers: your device advertising ID (e.g. Google Advertising ID) and approximate location derived from IP address, used to serve and measure ads.

2. Data stored on your device

Your game progress, coins, settings, selected skins, achievements, and sound/music preferences are stored locally on your device. This data stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

3. Third-party services

The app uses the following third-party services, which may collect data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob – shows advertising. policies.google.com/privacy
  • Firebase Analytics – anonymous usage statistics to help us improve the game. firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Firebase Crashlytics – crash reports to help us fix bugs.

4. Advertising

Worm Escape shows ads, including optional rewarded ads that give in-game bonuses (such as extra moves or revives). Ads may be personalized based on your advertising ID. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

5. Notifications

With your permission, the app may send local reminder notifications (for example daily challenge reminders). These are generated on your device; no personal data is sent to us for this. You can turn them off in the app settings or in your device settings at any time.

6. Children's privacy

Worm Escape is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

7. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the service providers listed above, to operate analytics, crash reporting, and advertising.

8. Data retention and deletion

Local game data is deleted when you uninstall the app. Analytics and crash data held by Firebase are retained according to Google's standard retention periods. To request deletion of any data associated with you, email us at the address below.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of your personal data. Contact us to exercise these rights.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest revision. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
